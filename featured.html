Featured Books

Here are a few book suggestions for readers who want to explore different stories and ideas.

The Little Prince

Author: Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

A classic story about friendship, imagination, and seeing the world differently.

Anne of Green Gables

Author: L. M. Montgomery

A story about a curious girl, growing up, and finding a place to belong.

Noli Me Tangere

Author: José Rizal

A well-known Filipino novel that explores society during the Spanish colonial period.

Reading Programs and Activities

Libraries may hold storytelling sessions, book discussions, reading challenges, and learning activities. Ask your local library about its upcoming programs.

A Small Reading Reminder

You do not have to finish a whole book in one sitting. Take your time, enjoy a few pages, and let reading become part of your day.
