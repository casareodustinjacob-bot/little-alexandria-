About Little Alexandria

Little Alexandria is a website that promotes local libraries in the Philippines. It helps people discover libraries they can visit and encourages everyone to enjoy reading.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding local libraries easier and to spread awareness about the value of public and community libraries.

Why Reading Matters

Learn New Things

Books help us gain knowledge and understand different topics.

Improve Creativity

Reading lets us imagine new places, people, and ideas.

Build Good Habits

Reading regularly can help improve focus and vocabulary.

Our Values

We want our website to be educational, welcoming, friendly, and helpful to local communities.
